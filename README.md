<h2>Project Descriptions</h2>
        <h3>Week 2 Task 1: Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard</h3>
        <p>This project involves the creation of a detailed sales and profit analysis dashboard. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, regional performance, and product category analysis. Key features include:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>Interactive charts and graphs</li>
            <li>Drill-down capabilities</li>
            <li>Dynamic filtering options</li>
            <li>Real-time data updates</li>
        </ul>
        <p><strong>Visualizations:</strong></p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Total Sales by Category:</strong> A bar chart showing sales for Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture categories across different segments (Corporate, Home Office).</li>
            <li><strong>Profit By Country:</strong> A map highlighting profits by country in Europe.</li>
            <li><strong>Sales and Profits Over Time:</strong> A line chart showing the sum of sales and profits over time from January to December.</li>
            <li><strong>Sum of Sales by Region and Ship Mode:</strong> A stacked area chart showing the sum of sales in different regions (Central, South, North) categorized by ship mode (Economy, Economy Plus, Immediate, Priority).</li>
        </ul>
